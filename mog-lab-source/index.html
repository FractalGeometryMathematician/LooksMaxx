PSL guide / tiers & scoring ↗

The guide supplies tier vocabulary and a bell-curve description, but no implementable scoring formula or calibration dataset. This app uses 1 + 0.08 × capped target fit for the heuristic rating, bounded to 1–9; 10 is reserved as theoretical. Transition labels fill gaps between the guide’s stated bands.

Pool mode maps your smoothed within-pool rank to a normal scale with center 5 and standard deviation 1, bounded to 1–9. Enabled score ceilings also limit the rating. This is a relative target-fit ranking of the imported pool, not a validated PSL or global population rating.

Structure-priority weights: jaw/cheek contours 35%, harmony 25%, eyes 25%, nose/lips 10%, balance 5%. These weights and ratio targets are app-authored. Hair, grooming, physique, personality and status do not contribute. The profile panel adds surface geometry when combined mode is enabled; internal bone projection remains unavailable.

What does the score actually measure? +

The score combines facial harmony, eye area, jaw/cheekbones, nose/lip proportions, and landmark balance. Up to 18 frontal measurements use targets for your selected preset. Harsh mode is the default: scores fall continuously with distance from the target, including inside the original broad bands. Original mode preserves the forgiving ranges. Scores are shown to one decimal in Harsh mode. Compare results only with the same mode, preset, weights and measurement availability. Open the measurement table to inspect every value, range, fit score and deduction from the total. Change the group weights to express your preferences. Hair and photo texture inform presentation tips and stay separate from the structural score. Percentiles require an imported reference pool with at least five compatible measured reports, rescored using the current settings. This ranks that pool, not the global population. Optional ceiling rules show every trigger and any cap deduction. The PSL guide mode uses separate guide-aligned tier boundaries and a documented 1–9 heuristic conversion. Optional pool normalization requires compatible measured reports. Raw target-fit mode retains the older game-tier mapping. Poor pose or expression suppresses the overall score.

Is this an “ideal beauty mask”? +

The default guide shows eyes, brows, nose, lips and the jaw outline. An optional φ guide remains available as a schematic comparison; φ no longer determines the overall score. Mark the middle of your visible hairline to measure full facial thirds. Without a mark, the upper third is unavailable rather than guessed. Neither guide is the Marquardt mask.

What cannot a front-facing photo measure? +

True jaw gonial angle, forward growth, nose/chin projection, cheekbone projection, bite alignment and bone structure require more than a frontal landmark map. We show frontal outlines and do not fabricate these side-profile measurements. The lateral panel measures surface angles, chin offset and nose–chin-line lip gaps. Its six scored traits feed the main rating when combined mode is enabled. SNA/SNB, verified Frankfort alignment, internal bone projection and tissue composition remain unavailable from these photos.

What about skin and facial fat? +

Texture is a rough cheek-image contrast estimate. Lighting, makeup, hair and camera sharpening can change it; it cannot diagnose skin conditions or measure skin health. Facial fullness describes the visible outline as rounder, moderately tapered, or more tapered using cheek-to-height and jaw-to-cheek ratios. It cannot distinguish facial fat from bone structure, pose or lens distortion. A poor-pose scan asks for a retake. Lower-cheek taper grades the visible contour against the chosen target. Tissue-fat percentage remains unavailable.

How does hairstyle detection work? +

A local model identifies the visible hair region. Rules suggest a broad outline: cropped, volume on top, fringe, or medium/long. Hats, cropped hair, very short hair, and lighting can make it unclear. Curl type and exact cuts require a manual correction.

Where does my photo go? +

Nowhere. The model and app load from this host, then your photo is processed locally in browser memory. Camera access starts only when you tap Use camera; no microphone is requested. The stream stops after capture or cancel, and when the tab is hidden. Clipboard images are read only when you paste or tap Paste image. No analytics, cookies, photo storage or external inference API. Clear your photo or close the tab to discard it. Downloads contain only the report you choose to save.
